Browser minesweeper

This is a recreation of classic minesweeper game made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. This project is supposed to showcase my abilities and knowledge on this part of webdevelopment. Hope you enjoy!

How to play the game

  • The goal of the game is to clear the entire board by flagging the right cells which include a mine (*icon of mine*).
  • After player creates a board, he can left click on cells to reveal themselves or right click them to flag them (*icon of flag*).
  • If a cell includes a number, the number represents the amount of mines included around the cell.
  • If the player clicks on a cell that is a mine, it's game over and the player has to restart the game.
  • The game ends when the player correctly flags all mines on the field.

Basic settings

Left click: Reveal cell

Right click: Flag cell

Board size and difficulty: Adjustments for the player's experience
